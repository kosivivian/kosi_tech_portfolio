How I work

Four steps,
one loop.

It's not about which tool to use. It's about what is worth building, and making sure your team actually gets the result.

  2. 01

    Audit

    I look into how things run today and ask the right questions, because the question isn't what tool to use. It's what is worth building to drive real impact.

  3. 02

    Plan

    I map the system, whether it's a model, an automation or backend work, and turn your messy information into a structured plan with the risks and budget written down.

    • Time saved
    • Money saved
    • Fewer mistakes & risks
  4. 03

    Build

    I build in small, testable pieces with you in the loop, so there are no surprises at handover and every part can be checked against the plan.

  5. 04

    Deliver

    I don't just deliver the technology and disappear. Adoption, training and change management are part of the job, so you get the result and feel confident running the system.
